GRAZZARK - a first-person ASCII roguelike

TO PLAY
  * From a terminal:   ./grazzark
  * Or double-click "grazzark" in your file manager. If it was not started
    from a terminal, the game opens itself in a terminal window
    (gnome-terminal, konsole, xfce4-terminal, kitty, alacritty, xterm, ...).
  * To add it to your applications menu: ./install-linux.sh

A terminal of at least 120x36 characters with true colour looks best.
On first launch you name your profile; with several profiles the game
asks which one to play. You can add or switch profiles from the title menu.
Options: --no-audio, --256 (for terminals without true colour), --help

Profiles (soul points, upgrades, bestiary, saved run) are kept in
~/.local/share/grazzark/profiles/
